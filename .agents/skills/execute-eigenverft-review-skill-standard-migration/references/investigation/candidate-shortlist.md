# 3.4 Candidate Shortlist — Prüfbare Kandidaten auswählen

**Responsibility:** Collect the surviving investigation-worthy questions and decide whether there are still important unexplored discovery directions.

During migration:
- Keep the concrete questions that survived refinement; remove duplicates and unsound broad parent labels.
- Reconsider whether the objective, review lens, and authorized scope suggest other material directions before proceeding to Evaluation.
- Treat the shortlist as a **working set of questions**, not a published artifact or a set of verified findings. No printed list, quota, formal queue, or fixed candidate count is required.
- Pass surviving candidates to Validation, Assessment, and Decisions. If none survives after adequate discovery, Evaluation and Reporting may still yield a legitimate no-finding result.
- Respond to new material leads discovered during Evaluation by returning selectively to Broad Discovery or Refinement rather than restarting an unbounded search.

**Result:** A relevant candidate set ready for deeper assessment and an informed view of remaining discovery coverage.
