# 4.3 Decisions — Schlussfolgerungen bestimmen

**Responsibility:** Decide which candidates qualify as reportable findings, which remain unresolved, and whether the review is sufficiently complete.

During migration:
- Define actionable outcome states suited to the review: supported finding/direction, limited or conditional observation, rejected concern, or no material issue.
- Avoid publishing rejected or unverified hypotheses as confident findings; do not force severity levels or minimum finding counts.
- Before Reporting, instruct the agent to reconsider relevant paths, counterarguments, ownership or behavior questions, and plausible lower-severity issues that may have been missed because higher-severity findings were found.
- **Completion decision:** If further meaningful evidence is available, return to Investigation and re-evaluate. If conclusions are sufficiently supported, proceed to Reporting, including a zero-finding result. If material gaps cannot be resolved, preserve and report them as limitations.
- Separate the decision to recommend work from permission to perform it.

**Result:** The report reflects an actual assessment and a deliberate completion decision rather than a first-finding stopping rule.
