# 4.3 Decisions

**Responsibility:** Decide which candidates merit reporting, which require further investigation, and what remains uncertain.

During migration:
- Distinguish supported findings or directions, conditional observations, rejected concerns, and no material issue.
- Explain the decision through available evidence and meaningful counterarguments. Treat severity as a property of the concern rather than a quota or stopping point.
- Revisit relevant in-scope questions that could still affect the result. Several Critical, High, or Medium findings should not prevent continued attention to worthwhile Low-severity concerns.
- When further discovery could reveal material issues, return to Investigation. When an answerable validation or assessment question remains, examine the necessary evidence. Otherwise report the supported conclusions, including zero findings.
- State concrete limitations when evidence is unavailable and the resulting judgment is necessarily bounded.
- Keep implementation recommendations separate from permission to execute them.

**Result:** A supported assessment with practical follow-up where useful, rather than a conclusion reached after the first plausible findings.
