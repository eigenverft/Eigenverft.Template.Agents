# 1.1 Identity

**Responsibility:** Establish the review skill's distinct identity, subject, and intended insight.

During migration:
- Keep a distinctive, accurate `name`, directory name, and clear skill title. Maintain the repository's actual frontmatter conventions.
- State what the review examines and the kind of conclusions it provides, in terms that distinguish it from neighboring review skills.
- Preserve the original domain-specific purpose. Do not broaden the skill into a general audit just to fit common terminology.
- Keep identity separate from the current user's particular review target, which belongs to Request Resolution and Target.

**Result:** A user and agent can recognize *which review capability this is* without reading its whole procedure.
