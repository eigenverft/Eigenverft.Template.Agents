# 2.2 Objective — Erkenntnisziel bestimmen

**Responsibility:** Define the question the review should answer and the improvement or understanding sought.

During migration:
- Express the review's specific intellectual purpose in positive, actionable terms: what to investigate, compare, assess, or explain.
- Keep the objective independent of the particular repository, file, or diff chosen as Target.
- Preserve the original review expertise, including its legitimate trade-offs and what makes a meaningful finding in that domain.
- State what *successfully answering the question* looks like without requiring any minimum number of findings.

**Result:** The agent knows what insight to produce, not merely which files to read.
