# 2.9 Method Contract — Prüfverfahren festlegen

**Responsibility:** Describe how the target review agent should investigate and evaluate, consistent with its objective, evidence access, and authority.

During migration:
- Keep investigation and evaluation methods distinct: discover evidence/candidates first; then test hypotheses, significance, counterarguments, and practical remedies.
- Preserve a review-specific discovery strategy if it works. Direct candidate investigation and tree-like discovery/refinement are alternatives. Do not merge variants or add quotas merely to simulate rigor.
- Prefer concrete positive instructions. Explain what to inspect, compare, verify, and revisit rather than relying only on generic "be thorough" language.
- For intensive finding-oriented reviews, explicitly direct a completion self-review: continue exploring material in-scope paths after initial findings, including relevant Low-severity concerns after Critical/High/Medium findings. Return to Investigation for meaningful unanswered questions before Reporting.
- Stop when further relevant work is unlikely to change the assessment or when evidence limits prevent resolution; report such limits. Require no fixed number of findings.
- Respect Authority: a useful method never grants permission to edit, build, run tools, or expand scope.

**Result:** The skill has a practical, non-mechanical investigation contract and a defensible route to completion.
