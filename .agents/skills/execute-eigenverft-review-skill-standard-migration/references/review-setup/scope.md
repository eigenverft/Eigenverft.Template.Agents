# 2.4 Scope

**Responsibility:** Determine what is in and out of the review's judgments, and what coverage is expected.

During migration:
- Honor explicitly narrowed or excluded areas from the user and applicable instructions.
- If no narrower scope is supplied, direct investigation across all relevant parts of the established Target under the skill's Objective and Evaluation Lens.
- Describe how a large target is explored systematically and when meaningful evidence or time/access limits must be disclosed.
- Separate review-judgment scope from the supporting evidence needed to understand context.
- Avoid a self-chosen tiny sample as a silent default and avoid claiming exhaustive coverage that was not achieved.

**Result:** Both the intended breadth and actual coverage boundaries can be stated accurately.
