# 5.5 Alternatives

**Responsibility:** Present meaningful alternative interpretations or improvement directions when they aid a decision.

During migration:
- Show options grounded in the actual domain, constraints, and affected responsibilities.
- Compare practical advantages, trade-offs, preserved guarantees, and relevant risks; include retaining the current approach when credible.
- Distinguish a partially designed direction from a complete implementation plan.
- Do not dump every internally considered option or turn a straightforward result into a mandatory design competition.

**Result:** Relevant choices remain visible without pretending every alternative is equally sound.
