# 5.6 Actions — Empfohlene Schritte

**Responsibility:** Suggest concrete next steps when the review supports further investigation, design, or implementation.

During migration:
- Tie each recommendation to a supported finding, material limitation, or unresolved decision.
- Prefer a bounded first step appropriate to the maturity of the evidence; a plausible direction may require design work before implementation.
- Separate suggested implementation order or urgency from the finding's severity.
- State dependencies and verification needs when material.
- Keep recommendations non-executable under the review's read-only Authority: reporting a step is not authorization to perform it.

**Result:** Actionable suggestions that leave acceptance and integration decisions with the user.
