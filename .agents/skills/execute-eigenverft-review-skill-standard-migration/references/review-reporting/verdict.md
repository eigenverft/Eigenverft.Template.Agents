# 5.3 Verdict — Zusammenfassende Beurteilung

**Responsibility:** Summarize an overall judgment when the review's purpose warrants one.

During migration:
- Decide whether an overall verdict is useful for this review type. Some reviews should present findings without a universal quality score.
- If used, anchor the verdict in reviewed scope, evidence, evaluation criteria, and substantive caveats.
- Separate overall judgment from severity-ranked individual findings and from a mere explanation of how something works.
- Do not imply full coverage or mathematical certainty when the review is limited or subjective.

**Result:** A concise, appropriately scoped conclusion when one adds real value.
