# 5.2 Findings — Qualifizierte Befunde

**Responsibility:** Present validated, meaningful review concerns in a form the reader can assess and act on.

During migration:
- Require a stable reference for each finding, a specific problem statement, concrete supporting evidence, practical significance, and an intelligible improvement direction where the review recommends changes.
- Group symptoms with the same underlying cause; keep independently selectable changes distinct.
- Make the severity appropriate to the observed concern, not to how many findings have already been identified.
- Keep unresolved hypotheses outside the confirmed findings overview and do not invent low-severity findings to fill a table.
- Support proportionate depth: an overview supports scanning; finding details let the reader judge the idea without treating the reviewer as an ultimate authority.
- The **severity-rated findings table and exact label convention** are specified in [5.8 Output Formatting & Delivery](output-formatting-delivery.md).

**Result:** Findings are evidence-supported possibilities for improvement, not claimed infallible truths or permission to implement.
