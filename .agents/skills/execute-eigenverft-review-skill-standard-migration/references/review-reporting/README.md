# 05 · Review Reporting

Select the outcome types that suit the review and present them according to its **output contract**, known throughout the review and applied at delivery.

- [5.1 Result Selection](result-selection.md) — choose applicable outcome types.
- [5.2 Findings](findings.md) — qualified concerns.
- [5.3 Verdict](verdict.md) — overall judgment when useful.
- [5.4 Explanation](explanation.md) — explanatory synthesis.
- [5.5 Alternatives](alternatives.md) — relevant options.
- [5.6 Actions](actions.md) — recommended next steps.
- [5.7 Limitations](limitations.md) — uncertainty and coverage boundaries.
- [5.8 Output Formatting & Delivery](output-formatting-delivery.md) — concrete Eigenverft presentation contract.

**Migration check:** A reader can obtain a quick overview and enough detail for decisions. Findings, verdicts, explanations, alternatives, and actions are **possible outcome types**, not mandatory sections in every report. A severity-rated findings review uses the Eigenverft severity table; an explanatory review does not invent findings just to fill it.
