# 5.7 Limitations

**Responsibility:** Explain material uncertainty, inaccessible evidence, and actual scope or coverage limits.

During migration:
- Report specific missing sources, unresolved questions, revision ambiguities, and areas not sufficiently examined when these affect conclusions.
- Separate lack of findings from proof that no issues exist.
- Offer a focused next investigation only when the missing evidence might materially change the assessment.
- Avoid both unjustified certainty and generic disclaimer sections that obscure otherwise clear results.

**Result:** The reader understands what the review did and did not establish.
