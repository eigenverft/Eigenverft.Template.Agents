# 5.4 Explanation

**Responsibility:** Explain relevant behavior, rationale, consequences, or relationships so the reader understands the review result.

During migration:
- Include a synthesis when the reader needs to understand why a finding matters, how a design works, or why certain trade-offs were considered.
- Ground explanation in inspected evidence and keep hypotheses identifiable.
- Prefer direct, understandable language before internal review jargon; keep deep details available after a fast overview.
- Do not manufacture defects or severity ratings simply because explanatory work produced no findings.

**Result:** A reader can follow the reasoning, not just the conclusion.
