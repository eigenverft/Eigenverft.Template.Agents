# Change Blast Radius

**Broad inspection, narrow modification, consequence-based stopping.**

- Anchor on the requested change and what constitutes its complete fulfillment.
- Trace direct and indirect consequences through relevant relationships and constraints that can plausibly transmit the change. Treat plausible impact as a reason to inspect; require verifiable necessity to modify.
- Make the smallest complete set of required changes, covering every verified necessary consequence without expanding into optional or speculative work.
- Recheck affected relationships and constraints after the change. Follow newly discovered consequences by the same standard, and stop when the intended change is fulfilled and no verified necessary consequence remains unresolved.
