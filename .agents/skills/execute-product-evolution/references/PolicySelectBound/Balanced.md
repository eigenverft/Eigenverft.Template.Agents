# Select & Bound — Balanced

Read [Contract.md](Contract.md) first. This strategy belongs to the **Balanced** cross-phase profile and can be independently refined with natural-language guidance.

## Selection Preference

Choose the strongest viable product increment whose added consumer value reasonably justifies moderate implementation and review costs. Complete workflow slices and cross-layer improvements are allowed when they express **one** recognizable outcome.

## Change Budget

Allow moderately wider effects than Conservative but require bounded semantic blast radius, sufficiently clear compatibility, feasible verification and a comprehensible review unit. Do not batch separate goals merely because files or components overlap.

## Handoff

Explain why the increment provides appropriate value for its complexity and document its real boundaries under [Contract.md](Contract.md).
