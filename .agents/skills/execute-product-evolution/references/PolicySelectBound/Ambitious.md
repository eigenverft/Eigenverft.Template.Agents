# Select & Bound — Ambitious

Read [Contract.md](Contract.md) first. This strategy belongs to the **Ambitious** cross-phase profile; it does not weaken eligibility gates or authorize breaking changes.

## Selection Preference

Prefer a strategically meaningful improvement when a genuinely **independently valuable** and cohesive increment can be isolated. A useful vertical slice, architectural simplification or substantial consumer workflow improvement may outrank a trivial cleanup.

## Change Budget

Accept more consequential but still comprehensible and reversible product changes where justified; preserve consumer contracts, bounded risk, acceptance criteria and feasible verification. Never turn a broad redesign into an unreviewable mega-change. If it cannot be sliced responsibly, choose another opportunity or No change.

## Handoff

State why the ambitious choice remains small **enough for human review**, along with expected outcomes and exclusions according to [Contract.md](Contract.md).
