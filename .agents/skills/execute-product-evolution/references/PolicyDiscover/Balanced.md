# Discover — Balanced

Read [Contract.md](Contract.md) first. This strategy belongs to the **Balanced** cross-phase profile; it remains independently overrideable by explicit natural-language intent.

## Search Priority

Survey existing workflows, consumer-facing friction, missing **practical** product capabilities and internal design/operability opportunities. Look beyond isolated defects where the product offers clear evidence for meaningful improvement, including connected workflow or cross-component opportunities.

## Candidate Quality

Include modest capability additions and workflow simplification alongside technical health improvements, but keep observation separate from hypotheses. Wider discovery does not authorize a wider implementation.

## Handoff

Return evidence-backed, comparable opportunities under [Contract.md](Contract.md), noting beneficiaries and trade-offs for Assess.
