# Discover — Ambitious

Read [Contract.md](Contract.md) first. This strategy belongs to the **Ambitious** cross-phase profile; it never relaxes shared consumer contracts or authorization.

## Search Priority

Explore strategic product gaps, potentially valuable new capabilities, conceptual simplifications, underserved consumer workflows and long-term evolution opportunities, alongside existing friction and reliability problems. A larger exploration horizon can legitimately identify opportunities too broad for this run.

## Candidate Quality

Explicitly classify forward-looking opportunities as hypotheses where consumer evidence is incomplete. Map potential benefits, impacted consumer groups and key assumptions without treating novelty or strategic ambition as proof of value.

## Handoff

Provide evidence and uncertainty for Assess; do not silently select an oversized initiative or assume that a conceptual redesign can be implemented as one increment.
