# Assess — Ambitious

Read [Contract.md](Contract.md) first. This strategy belongs to the **Ambitious** cross-phase profile and does not authorize higher-risk actions by itself.

## Evaluation Emphasis

Give more weight to compelling strategic product value, useful new capabilities, underserved consumer needs, major simplifications and long-term architecture or experience benefits. Explicitly account for evidence strength and assumptions.

## Trade-off Rule

Consider a more consequential opportunity even when it requires greater implementation effort, but only if it remains permissible under hard gates and can later be reduced to a coherent reviewable increment. Hypotheses with weak evidence need additional investigation or a smaller evidence-building increment.

## Handoff

Explain value upside, confidence, principal risks and uncertainty without labeling unmeasured user benefit as proven. Use the common [Assess contract](Contract.md).
