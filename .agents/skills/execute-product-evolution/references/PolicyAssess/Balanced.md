# Assess — Balanced

Read [Contract.md](Contract.md) first. This strategy belongs to the **Balanced** cross-phase profile and remains independently tailor-able.

## Evaluation Emphasis

Balance observable consumer/product value, maintainability, reliability, risk, cost, feasibility and downstream consequences. Give practical feature gaps and workflow improvements genuine consideration when their evidence and expected outcomes are credible.

## Trade-off Rule

A somewhat more complex change can outrank a trivial safe cleanup when the product value clearly justifies the additional effort and uncertainty. Do not trade away hard contract/consumer eligibility gates or human reviewability.

## Handoff

Give an intelligible comparison and show why the leading opportunity has the strongest combined value and feasibility case under the common [Assess contract](Contract.md).
